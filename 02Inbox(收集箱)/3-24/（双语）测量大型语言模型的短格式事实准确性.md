# Measuring short-form factuality in large language models  
测量大型语言模型的短格式事实准确性

Jason Wei∗          Nguyen Karina1        Hyung Won Chung        Yunxin Joy Jiao         
Spencer Papay        Amelia Glaese        John Schulman        William FedusOpenAI

###### Abstract  摘要

We present SimpleQA, a benchmark that evaluates the ability of language models to answer short, fact-seeking questions. We prioritized two properties in designing this eval. First, SimpleQA is challenging, as it is adversarially collected against GPT-4 responses. Second, responses are easy to grade, because questions are created such that there exists only a single, indisputable answer. Each answer in SimpleQA is graded as either correct, incorrect, or not attempted. A model with ideal behavior would get as many questions correct as possible while not attempting the questions for which it is not confident it knows the correct answer. SimpleQA is a simple, targeted evaluation for whether models “know what they know,” and our hope is that this benchmark will remain relevant for the next few generations of frontier models. SimpleQA can be found at [https://github.com/openai/simple-evals](https://github.com/openai/simple-evals)  
我们提出了 SimpleQA，这是一个评估语言模型回答简短、寻求事实问题能力的基准。在设计这个评估时，我们优先考虑了以下两个特性。首先，SimpleQA 具有挑战性，因为它是针对 GPT-4 的回应进行对抗性收集的。其次，回应易于评分，因为问题的设计使得每个问题只有一个无可争议的答案。SimpleQA 中的每个答案被评分为正确、错误或未尝试。理想行为的模型应尽可能多地答对问题，同时不尝试那些它不确定自己知道正确答案的问题。SimpleQA 是一个简单、有针对性的评估，用于判断模型是否“知道它们知道什么”，我们希望这个基准在未来的几代前沿模型中仍然保持相关性。SimpleQA 可在 https://github.com/openai/simple-evals 找到.

## 1Introduction  1 引言

An open problem in artificial intelligence is how to train language models that produce responses that are factually correct. Current frontier models sometimes produce false outputs or answers that are not substantiated by evidence, a problem known as “hallucinations.” Such hallucinations are one of the major barriers for broader adoption of general forms artificial intelligence like large language models.  
人工智能领域的一个开放性问题是如何训练语言模型，使其生成符合事实的回应。当前的前沿模型有时会产生错误的输出或缺乏证据支持的答案，这一问题被称为"幻觉"。此类幻觉是阻碍大型语言模型等通用人工智能形式更广泛应用的主要障碍之一。

Factuality is a complicated topic because it is hard to measure—evaluating the factuality of any given arbitrary claim can be challenging, and language models often generate long completions that contain dozens of factual claims. In this work we will sidestep the open-endedness of language models by considering only short, fact-seeking questions with a single answer. This reduction of scope is important because it makes measuring factuality much more tractable, albeit at the cost of leaving open research questions such as whether improved behavior on short-form factuality generalizes to long-form factuality.  
事实性是一个复杂的议题，因为它难以衡量——评估任意给定主张的事实性可能具有挑战性，而语言模型通常生成包含数十个事实主张的长篇补全内容。在这项工作中，我们将通过仅考虑具有单一答案的简短事实查询问题，来规避语言模型的开放性。这种范围缩减很重要，因为它使衡量事实性变得更容易处理，尽管代价是留下了一些开放的研究问题，例如在短篇事实性方面的改进行为是否能够推广到长篇事实性。

We present a benchmark called SimpleQA, which contains 4,326 short, fact-seeking questions. SimpleQA was designed with a few important properties in mind:  
我们提出了一个名为 SimpleQA 的基准测试，其中包含 4,326 个简短的事实查询问题。SimpleQA 在设计时考虑了以下几个重要特性：

- • 
    
    High correctness. Reference answers to questions are determined by two independent AI trainers, and questions were written in such a way that the predicted answers are easily gradable.
    
      
    • 高准确性。问题的参考答案由两位独立的 AI 训练师确定，且问题设计方式使得预测答案易于评分。
- • 
    
    Good researcher UX. SimpleQA is fast and simple to run, as questions and answers are very short. Grading is also fast to run via the OpenAI API (or another frontier model API). Additionally, with 4,326 questions in the dataset, SimpleQA should have relatively low run-to-run variance.
    
      
    • 良好的研究者体验。SimpleQA 运行快速且操作简便，因为问题与答案都非常简短。通过 OpenAI API（或其他前沿模型 API）进行评分同样高效。此外，数据集包含 4,326 个问题，SimpleQA 的运行间方差应相对较低。
- • 
    
    Challenging for frontier models. Compared to older benchmarks such as TriviaQA (Joshi et al., [2017](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib4)) or Natural Questions (Kwiatkowski et al., [2019](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib8)) that are now saturated, SimpleQA is created to be challenging for frontier models (e.g., GPT-4o and Claude both score less than 50%).
    
      
    • 对前沿模型具有挑战性。相较于已趋于饱和的旧有基准测试（如 TriviaQA（Joshi 等人，2017 年）或 Natural Questions（Kwiatkowski 等人，2019 年）），SimpleQA 专为挑战前沿模型而设计（例如 GPT-4o 和 Claude 的得分均低于 50%）。
- • 
    
    Diversity. SimpleQA contains questions from a wide range of topics, including history, science & technology, art, geography, TV shows, etc.
    
      
    • 多样性。SimpleQA 涵盖广泛主题的问题，包括历史、科学技术、艺术、地理、电视节目等。

## 2Data collection and verification  
2 数据收集与验证

Data collection for SimpleQA was done in two stages. First, AI trainers (i.e., human annotators) created question and answer pairs. Then, questions were independently answered by another AI trainer and only kept if answers from both trainers matched.  
SimpleQA 的数据收集分为两个阶段。首先，人工智能训练师（即人类标注员）创建问答对。然后，这些问题由另一位人工智能训练师独立回答，只有当两位训练师的答案一致时，这些问题才会被保留。

### 2.1Question and answer criteria  
2.1 问答标准

To create the dataset, we asked AI trainers to create knowledge-seeking questions that fit a very specific set of criteria.  
为了创建数据集，我们要求人工智能训练师创建符合特定标准的知识寻求型问题。

Must have a single answer. Here we only focus on objective knowledge and force questions to be written such that they only have a single, indisputable answer. One part of this criterion that the question must specify the scope of the answer. For example, instead of asking “where did Barack and Michelle Obama meet” (for which could have multiple answers “Chicago” or “the law firm Sidley & Austin”), questions had to specify “which city” or “which company.” Another common example is that instead of asking simply “when,” questions had to ask “what year” or “what date.”  
必须只有一个答案。在此我们仅关注客观知识，并强制要求问题必须以仅有一个无可争议的答案的方式编写。这一标准的一部分是问题必须明确答案的范围。例如，不应问“巴拉克和米歇尔·奥巴马在哪里相遇”（可能有多个答案，如“芝加哥”或“律师事务所 Sidley & Austin”），而必须明确问“哪个城市”或“哪家公司”。另一个常见例子是，不应简单地问“何时”，而必须问“哪一年”或“哪一天”。

Reference answers should not change over time. To keep this dataset evergreen, questions are written so that their answers would not change over time, which can require increasing the degree of specificity. For example, instead of broadly asking “who is Meredith’s partner in Grey’s Anatomy,” which could change as new seasons are produced, questions asking about TV shows, movies, video games, and sports typically required specifying a point in time (e.g., “who is Meredith’s partner in Grey’s Anatomy in Season 13”). However, we disallowed questions that tacked on “as of 2023,” which would make the dataset somewhat contrived.  
参考答案不应随时间改变。为确保数据集长期有效，问题的设计需使其答案不随时间变化，这通常需要提高问题的具体程度。例如，与其宽泛地询问“《实习医生格蕾》中梅雷迪思的伴侣是谁”（该答案可能随新季播出而改变），涉及电视剧、电影、电子游戏和体育的问题通常需要指定时间节点（例如“《实习医生格蕾》第 13 季中梅雷迪思的伴侣是谁”）。但我们禁止使用“截至 2023 年”这类附加条件的问题，以免使数据集显得刻意。

Reference answers must be supported by evidence. When AI trainers initially created a question and reference answer, they were also asked to provide a link to the webpage that supports the reference answer to the question. All questions then went through a second annotation stage where another AI trainer independently answered the question. Only questions where the answers from both AI trainers were the same were kept in the dataset.  
参考答案必须有证据支持。当 AI 训练师最初创建问题及参考答案时，他们还需提供支持该答案的网页链接。所有问题随后会进入第二轮标注阶段，由另一名 AI 训练师独立作答。仅当两名 AI 训练师的答案完全一致时，该问题才会被保留在数据集中。

Must be challenging. When trainers created questions, they would also review responses from four OpenAI models. The trainers had to classify each of the cour completions as correct, incorrect, or not attempted. At least one of the four completions must be incorrect for the trainer to continue with that question; otherwise, the trainer was instructed to create a new question. For most of the data creation process, all four completion came from GPT-4 models of various release dates. Towards the end, we changed one model to GPT-3.5, so that questions would be slightly easier and SimpleQA to give some signal on smaller models.  
这一定很有挑战性。当训练师创建问题时，他们还需要审阅四个 OpenAI 模型的回答。训练师必须将每个模型生成的答案分类为正确、错误或未尝试。只有当至少有一个模型的回答是错误的，训练师才会继续处理该问题；否则，训练师会被要求创建一个新问题。在数据创建的大部分过程中，所有四个答案都来自不同发布日期的 GPT-4 模型。在接近尾声时，我们将其中一个模型改为 GPT-3.5，这样问题会稍微简单一些，而 SimpleQA 则用于为较小的模型提供一些信号。

The question must be answerable as of 2023. Finally, we required questions to be answerable as of December 31, 2023, so that we could equally evaluate all models trained with data knowledge cutoffs up to that date.  
问题必须能在 2023 年得到解答。最后，我们要求问题在 2023 年 12 月 31 日之前是可回答的，这样我们就能公平地评估所有基于该日期之前数据知识截止点训练的模型。

### 2.2Data quality  2.2 数据质量

We took several steps to improve the quality of questions written by AI trainers. First, during the question creation stage, we ran a series of few-shot-prompted ChatGPT classifiers to detect criteria violations such as not specifying units, having answers that change over time, or having multiple answers. The questions with violations detected by ChatGPT were sent back to AI trainers for revision. At the end of the question creation stage, we used ChatGPT to lightly re-write questions to improve grammar and punctuation without modifying the content of the question.  
我们采取多项措施提升 AI 训练师所编写问题的质量。首先，在问题创建阶段，我们运行了一系列基于少量示例提示的 ChatGPT 分类器，用于检测违反标准的情况，例如未指定单位、答案随时间变化或存在多个答案。被 ChatGPT 检测出违规的问题会退回给 AI 训练师进行修改。在问题创建阶段结束时，我们使用 ChatGPT 对问题进行轻度改写，以改进语法和标点符号，同时不改变问题的内容。

In the verification stage of this dataset, each question was answered by an independent AI trainer without access to the answer given by the question creator. In this stage we also had AI trainers answer yes/no questions for whether questions only had single, indisputable answers, and whether answers to questions would stay the same over time.  
在该数据集的验证阶段，每个问题均由独立的 AI 训练师回答，且无法查看问题创建者提供的答案。在此阶段，我们还让 AI 训练师回答是否类问题，以判断问题是否仅具有单一且无可争议的答案，以及问题的答案是否会随时间保持不变。

After the verification stage, we removed questions from the dataset if answers from two trainers didn’t agree according to a prompted ChatGPT classifier, or if the second trainer said the question wasn’t timeless or didn’t have an indisputable answer. To improve the correctness of reference answers, we also only kept questions for which there were two unique website domain names among the 2–4 sources found by the two AI trainers (one source from the first AI trainer, and 1–3 from the second AI trainer).  
在验证阶段后，我们根据提示的 ChatGPT 分类器，若两位训练师的答案不一致，或第二位训练师认为问题不具备永恒性、或不存在无可争议的答案，则从数据集中移除这些问题。为提高参考答案的准确性，我们还仅保留了两位 AI 训练师找到的 2-4 个来源中（第一位 AI 训练师提供一个来源，第二位提供 1-3 个来源）包含两个不同网站域名的问题。

After the dataset was finalized with reference answers agreed on by two trainers, we did an additional quality check by randomly selecting 1,000 examples and having them answered by a third trainer. According to the prompted ChatGPT grader, the performance of the third trainer was 94.4%. Of the 5.6% of answers (56/1000) classified as incorrect by the prompted ChatGPT grader, we did a manual examination of all examples and found that fifteen were false negatives from the autograder. Of the remaining 41 (4.1%) actually incorrect answers, we found that seven were due to the third trainer not answering the question fully (e.g., giving only the year when the question asks for month and year), and six were due to the third trainer giving an answer that contradicted the source they cited (e.g., they misread the own source they cited).  
在数据集最终确定并得到两位训练师认可的参考答案后，我们通过随机选取 1000 个示例，由第三位训练师进行回答，进行了额外的质量检查。根据提示的 ChatGPT 评分器，第三位训练师的表现达到了 94.4%。在被提示的 ChatGPT 评分器归类为错误的 5.6%答案（56/1000）中，我们对所有示例进行了人工检查，发现其中有 15 个是自动评分器的假阴性错误。在剩余的 41 个（4.1%）实际错误答案中，我们发现 7 个是由于第三位训练师未完全回答问题（例如，问题要求提供月份和年份，但只给出了年份），6 个是由于第三位训练师给出的答案与他们引用的来源相矛盾（例如，他们误读了自己引用的来源）。

The remaining 2.8% of the errors from the third trainer revealed real issues with the data. Hence, we estimate that the error rate of our benchmark is around 3%, assuming no false positives from the prompted ChatGPT grader. The most common issues were ambiguous questions (e.g., not specifying driving vs flying distance between cities), reputable sources giving contradictory information (e.g., history.com and wikipedia.com give different dates for when Nixon retired from the US Naval Reserve), and having more than one correct answer (e.g., John Lennon’s psychedelic Rolls-Royce was shown at the Pacific National Exhibition in two years: 2014 and 2015).  
第三位训练员发现的剩余 2.8%错误揭示了数据中的真实问题。因此，我们估计基准测试的错误率约为 3%，前提是提示型 ChatGPT 评分器没有误判。最常见的问题包括：模糊不清的提问（例如未明确说明城市间的驾车距离与飞行距离）、权威来源提供矛盾信息（例如 history.com 和 wikipedia.com 对尼克松从美国海军预备役退役的日期记载不一），以及存在多个正确答案（例如约翰·列侬的迷幻劳斯莱斯汽车曾在两个年份的太平洋国家展览会上展出：2014 年和 2015 年）。

In addition to question topic, we can also look at the diversity of data in a few other axes. 除了问题主题外，我们还可以从其他几个维度考察数据的多样性。通过 ChatGPT 对答案类型进行分类，我们发现 32.8%的答案为日期，24.1%的答案涉及人物，15.3%的答案为数字，9.9%的答案为地点，另有 18.0%的答案被归类为"其他"。在来源多样性方面，wikipedia.com 是目前最大的信息来源（4300 个问题中有 3500 个将其作为来源之一），其次是 fandom.com（410 个问题）、ac.uk（154 个问题）和 imdb.com（121 个问题）。

为了对完成情况进行评分，我们使用了一个基于提示的 ChatGPT 分类器，该分类器同时查看预测答案和参考答案，并将回答评为“正确”、“错误”或“未尝试”。这些评级的定义以及一些示例回答如下表 2 所示.

We did not do a formal study of the performance of the grader but in practice, we found that it works pretty well. Of 100 correct, 100 incorrect, and 100 not attempted completions we manually read, we only found two disagreements with the prompted grader.  
完整的评分提示可在附录 A 中查看。我们未对评分器的性能进行正式研究，但在实践中发现其表现相当出色。在我们手动阅读的 100 个正确、100 个错误和 100 个未尝试的回答中，仅发现两处与提示评分器存在分歧的情况。

For any evaluation benchmark, having a single-number metric can be of great utility, even if it is imperfect. One way to do this is to first summarize “correct”, “incorrect”, or “not attempted” into two metrics that can be thought of as similar to (but not exactly the same as) recall and precision:  
对于任何评估基准而言，拥有一个单一数值指标都极具实用价值，即使它并不完美。实现这一点的一种方法是：首先将"正确"、"错误"或"未尝试"归纳为两个指标，这两个指标可类比为（但并非完全等同于）召回率与精确率：

- • 
    
    A metric called overall correct (or just “correct”) is simply what percent of all questions were answered correctly.
    
      
    • 名为"总体正确率"（或简称"正确率"）的指标，直接反映所有问题中回答正确的百分比。
- • 
    
    A metric called correct given attempted is what percent of questions the model answered correctly, out of only questions that were attempted (i.e., questions answered correct and incorrectly).
    
      
    • 名为"尝试正确率"的指标，则仅针对模型尝试回答的问题（即回答正确与错误的问题），计算其中回答正确的百分比。

To get a single-number metric, we can compute an F-score as the harmonic mean of overall correct and correct given attempted. To give a sense of how F-score captures precision and recall in this case, a model that always attempts to answer and gets 30% correct would get an F-score of 30%; a model that has correct-given-attempted of 80% would only need to get overall-correct of 19% to get an F-score of 30%. However, an issue with F-score is that if model performance is below 50%, it always makes sense for the model to try to guess if it is at least 50% sure that it will get an answer correct (see [Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#A2 "Appendix B Guessing strategy and F-score ‣ Measuring short-form factuality in large language models") for why this is the case; Kalai ([2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib6))).  
为了获得一个单一数值指标，我们可以计算 F 分数作为总体正确率和尝试正确率的调和平均数。为了说明 F 分数在这种情况下如何捕捉精确率和召回率：一个总是尝试回答且正确率为 30%的模型，其 F 分数为 30%；而一个尝试正确率达到 80%的模型，只需达到 19%的总体正确率即可获得 30%的 F 分数。然而，F 分数存在一个问题：如果模型性能低于 50%，那么只要模型至少有 50%的把握能给出正确答案，尝试猜测总是合理的（具体原因参见附录 B；Kalai 2024）。

A single-number metric that does not have a loophole would be to assign a specific negative penalty p to wrong answers, and then simply to take the average score where correct is worth 1 point, not attempted is worth 0 points, and incorrect is worth −p points. This metric is useful if one is willing to set a somewhat arbitrary threshold for −p for their particular use case. This metric can be interpreted as how much better a model is at getting answers correct than incorrect, with respect to the threshold. At p=9, the weighted sum of the scores for a model would only be positive if the model was getting at least 90% of the problems it attempted correct (a bar that none of the models we evaluate in this paper currently meet).  
一种不存在漏洞的单一数值指标，是为错误答案设定特定的负惩罚值 p ，然后直接计算平均得分——正确答案计 1 分，未尝试作答计 0 分，错误答案计 −p 分。若使用者愿意针对具体应用场景设定一个相对任意的阈值 −p ，该指标将具有实用价值。该指标可解读为：相对于设定阈值，模型给出正确答案的能力优于错误答案的程度。当阈值设为 p=9 时，模型得分加权总和仅在至少答对其尝试题目中 90%的情况下才为正值（本文评估的所有模型目前均未达到此标准）。

## 4Measuring calibration  4 测量校准度

A factuality benchmark like SimpleQA allows us to measure the scientific phenomenon known as calibration, or whether language models “know what they know.” One way to measure calibration is to directly ask the language model to state its confidence in its answer using a prompt like: “Please give your best guess, along with your confidence as a percentage that that is the correct answer” (for exact prompt, see [Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#A3 "Appendix C Calibration Prompt ‣ Measuring short-form factuality in large language models")). Then we can plot the correlation between the stated confidence of the model, and how accurate the model actually was. A perfectly calibrated model would have the same actual accuracy as stated confidence. For instance, on all prompts where the model stated a confidence of 75%, the accuracy would be 75% for a perfectly calibrated model.  
像 SimpleQA 这样的真实性基准测试，使我们能够衡量被称为校准度的科学现象，即语言模型是否“了解自身所知”。测量校准度的一种方法是直接要求语言模型通过提示语（例如：“请给出你的最佳猜测，并附上你对该答案正确性的置信度百分比”）来陈述其答案的置信度（具体提示语见附录 C）。然后，我们可以绘制模型陈述的置信度与实际准确率之间的相关性图。一个完美校准的模型的实际准确率将与其陈述的置信度相同。例如，在所有模型陈述置信度为 75%的提示中，完美校准模型的准确率应为 75%。

This result is shown in [Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#S4.F2 "Figure 2 ‣ 4 Measuring calibration ‣ Measuring short-form factuality in large language models") (left). The positive correlation between stated confidence and accuracy is a reassuring sign that models have some notion of confidence. We see that o1-preview is more calibrated than o1-mini, and gpt4o is more calibrated than gpt4o-mini, which is consistent with prior work showing that larger models are more calibrated. However, the fact that performance is well below the line y=x means that models consistently overstate their confidence. Hence, there is a lot of room to improve the calibration of large language models in terms of stated confidence.  
该结果如图 2（左）所示。陈述置信度与准确性之间的正相关是一个令人安心的迹象，表明模型具备一定的置信度概念。我们发现 o1-preview 比 o1-mini 具有更好的校准性，gpt4o 比 gpt4o-mini 的校准性更优，这与先前研究中“更大模型具有更好校准性”的结论一致。然而，实际表现远低于 y=x 基准线的事实表明，模型始终存在高估自身置信度的倾向。因此，大型语言模型在陈述置信度方面的校准能力仍有巨大提升空间。

![Refer to caption](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1/x1.png)

Figure 2:Left: Calibration of language models based on their stated confidence, uniformly binned into 15 intervals. Right: Calibration assessed by asking models the same question 100 times, quantile-binned into 30 intervals.  
图 2：左图：基于语言模型陈述置信度的校准，均匀划分为 15 个区间。右图：通过向模型重复提问 100 次评估校准效果，按分位数划分为 30 个区间。

Another way to measure calibration is to ask the language model the same question 100 times (here, we use temperature 1). Since language models may produce different answers upon repeated attempts, we can assess whether frequency of an answer corresponds to its correctness. Higher frequency typically indicates that the model is more confident in its answers, as the model is giving the same answer repeatedly. A calibrated model would have the same accuracy as answer frequency.  
另一种衡量校准效果的方法是让语言模型对同一问题回答 100 次（此处我们使用温度为 1 的参数设置）。由于语言模型在重复尝试中可能产生不同答案，我们可以评估答案出现频率是否与其正确性相对应。更高的频率通常意味着模型对其答案更具信心，因为模型在反复给出相同答案。经过校准的模型应当使答案准确率与出现频率保持一致。

In the [Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#S4.F2 "Figure 2 ‣ 4 Measuring calibration ‣ Measuring short-form factuality in large language models") (right), we show the calibration of language models as measured by the frequency of their responses. Here we use string match to group together different answers from the language model using the same prompt as the stated confidence figure. For each question, we only consider the most-frequent answer. We see across all models that accuracy increases with frequency, and that o1-preview has the highest level of calibration, where the frequency of the response is roughly equivalent to the accuracy of the response. Similar to calibration via stated confidence plot above, we again see o1-preview is more calibrated than o1-mini, and gpt4o is more calibrated than gpt4o-mini.  
在图 2（右侧）中，我们展示了语言模型根据其回答频率衡量的校准情况。此处我们采用字符串匹配方法，将语言模型对同一置信度提示给出的不同答案进行分组。针对每个问题，我们仅考虑最高频的答案。我们观察到所有模型的准确率均随频率提升而增长，其中 o1-preview 具有最高校准水平——其回答频率与回答准确率大致相当。与前述基于声明置信度的校准图类似，我们再次发现 o1-preview 比 o1-mini 校准程度更高，gpt4o 也比 gpt4o-mini 校准程度更高。

## 5Related work and discussion  
5 相关研究与讨论

In this paper we have proposed a very simple benchmark for measuring the factuality of language models. SimpleQA follows several prior benchmarks that aim to measure the ability of language models to provide knowledge about the world. Perhaps the two most similar benchmarks are TriviaQA (Joshi et al., [2017](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib4)) and Natural Questions (Kwiatkowski et al., [2019](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib8)), which were good datasets at the time but are too easy for today’s language models. Other recent and related benchmarks include LongFact (Wei et al., [2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib18)), a benchmark of open-ended prompts, and FreshQA (Vu et al., [2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib16)), a QA benchmark to evaluate performance on fast-changing knowledge, among other work (Lin et al., [2022a](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib10), Li et al., [2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib9), Cheng et al., [2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib3), Min et al., [2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib12), Zhao et al., [2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib19), Krishna et al., [2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib7))  
本文提出了一种非常简单的基准测试方法，用于衡量语言模型的事实准确性。SimpleQA 遵循了多个旨在衡量语言模型提供世界知识能力的先前基准测试。或许最相似的两个基准是 TriviaQA（Joshi 等人，2017 年）和 Natural Questions（Kwiatkowski 等人，2019 年），这些在当时是优秀的数据集，但对当今的语言模型来说过于简单。其他近期相关基准包括 LongFact（Wei 等人，2024 年）——一个开放式提示的基准测试，以及 FreshQA（Vu 等人，2023 年）——一个评估快速变化知识性能的问答基准测试，此外还有 Lin 等人（2022a）、Li 等人（2023 年）、Cheng 等人（2023 年）、Min 等人（2023 年）、Zhao 等人（2024 年）、Krishna 等人（2024 年）等研究。.

In our brief experiments we also measured calibration in language models, building on a long history of prior work studying whether neural nets are calibrated. Notably, Kadavath et al. ([2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib5)) studied calibration in language models, finding that they were increasingly calibrated as a function of training compute, where calibration is measured by the having the language model give the probability of a true statement. Our finding that frequency of answer correlates with accuracy is also consistent with Wang et al. ([2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib17)), which found a similar result for a different model on a math word problem benchmark. Other work has attempted to improve the calibration of language models (Lin et al., [2022b](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib11), Agrawal et al., [2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04368v1#bib.bib1)), which could lead to increased adoption of language models in real-world settings.  
在我们简短的实验中，我们还测量了语言模型的校准度，这建立在长期以来研究神经网络是否经过校准的先前工作基础上。值得注意的是，Kadavath 等人（2022 年）研究了语言模型的校准度，发现随着训练计算量的增加，它们的校准度逐渐提高，其中校准度是通过让语言模型给出真实陈述的概率来衡量的。我们发现答案频率与准确性相关的结论也与 Wang 等人（2023 年）的研究一致，他们在数学文字问题基准测试中对不同模型得出了类似的结果。其他研究尝试改进语言模型的校准度（Lin 等人，2022b；Agrawal 等人，2024 年），这可能有助于语言模型在现实场景中得到更广泛的应用。

A main limitation with SimpleQA is that while it is accurate, it only measures factuality under the constrained setting of short, fact-seeking queries with a single, verifiable answer. Whether the ability to provide factual short answers correlates with the ability to write lengthy responses filled with numerous facts remains an open research question. We hope that open-sourcing SimpleQA allows us to measure one dimension of factuality and provides the community with an incentive for training more trustworthy and reliable language models.  
SimpleQA 的主要局限在于，虽然它很准确，但仅在受约束的设置下衡量事实性，即针对简短、寻求事实的查询，且只有一个可验证的答案。提供事实性简短答案的能力是否与撰写包含大量事实的长篇回复的能力相关，这仍是一个开放的研究问题。我们希望开源 SimpleQA 能够让我们衡量事实性的一个维度，并为社区提供激励，以训练更值得信赖和可靠的语言模型。

## Appendix BGuessing strategy and F-score  
附录 B 猜测策略与 F 值

While F-score is a good metric in some ways, the issue with it is that it incentivizes the model to always guess when it is at least 50% sure that it can get the correct answer. To understand why this is the case, consider the following expression for the F-score:  
尽管 F 值在某些方面是一个不错的指标，但它的问题在于会激励模型在至少有 50%把握能得到正确答案时总是进行猜测。要理解为何如此，请看以下 F 值的表达式：

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
||F⁢-score=2c+ic+c+i+nc=2⁢c2⁢c+2⁢i+n,||

where:  其中：

- • 
    
    c is the number of correct answers,
    
      
    • c 是正确答案的数量，
- • 
    
    i is the number of incorrect answers, and
    
      
    • i 表示错误答案的数量，且
- • 
    
    n is the number of non-answered questions.
    
      
    • n 表示未回答问题的数量。

If you have a greater than 1/2 chance of being correct, your expected score from guessing is better than the score from not guessing, regardless of the specific values for c, i, and n. This is because the following inequality always holds:  
如果你的正确率超过 1/2 ，那么无论 c 、 i 和 n 的具体数值如何，通过猜测获得的预期分数都会高于不猜测的分数。这是因为以下不等式始终成立：

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
||2⁢c+12⁢c+2⁢i+n+2>2⁢c2⁢c+2⁢i+n+1.||

The left-hand side represents the expected F-score from guessing, assuming a 50/50 chance of correctness, while the right-hand side is the score from not answering the additional question. Since the denominators are adjusted similarly whether the guess is correct or incorrect, guessing with a probability >1/2 yields a better score.  
左侧代表在假设正确率为 50/50 的情况下通过猜测获得的预期 F 分数，而右侧是不回答额外问题时的分数。由于无论猜测正确与否，分母的调整方式相似，因此以 >1/2 的概率进行猜测会得到更高的分数。
