# Abstract  摘要

Automated reasoning with unstructured natural text is a key requirement for many potential applications of NLP and for developing robust AI systems. Recently, Language Models (LMs) have demonstrated complex reasoning capacities even without any finetuning. However, existing evaluation for automated reasoning assumes access to a consistent and coherent set of information over which models reason. When reasoning in the real-world, the available information is frequently inconsistent or contradictory, and therefore models need to be equipped with a strategy to resolve such conflicts when they arise. One widely-applicable way of resolving conflicts is to impose preferences over information sources (e.g., based on source credibility or information recency) and adopt the source with higher preference. In this paper, we formulate the problem of reasoning with contradictory information guided by preferences over sources as the classical problem of defeasible reasoning, and develop a dataset called BoardgameQA for measuring the reasoning capacity of LMs in this setting. BoardgameQA also incorporates reasoning with implicit background knowledge, to better reflect reasoning problems in downstream applications. We benchmark various LMs on BoardgameQA and the results reveal a significant gap in the reasoning capacity of state-of-the-art LMs on this problem, showing that reasoning with conflicting information does not surface out-of-the-box in LMs. While performance can be improved with finetuning, it nevertheless remains poor.  
对非结构化自然文本进行自动推理，是许多自然语言处理潜在应用及开发稳健人工智能系统的关键要求。近期，语言模型（LMs）即便未经任何微调，也展现出了复杂的推理能力。然而，现有的自动推理评估均假设模型能够获取一组一致且连贯的信息作为推理基础。在现实世界的推理场景中，可用信息常常存在不一致或相互矛盾的情况，因此模型需要具备在冲突出现时解决这些矛盾的策略。一种广泛适用的解决冲突方法是对信息源施加偏好（例如基于来源可信度或信息时效性），并采纳具有更高偏好的来源。本文将以信息源偏好为指导的矛盾信息推理问题，形式化为经典的可废止推理问题，并构建了一个名为 BoardgameQA 的数据集，用于衡量语言模型在此类情境下的推理能力。 BoardgameQA 还融入了对隐含背景知识的推理，以更好地反映下游应用中的推理问题。我们在 BoardgameQA 上对多种语言模型进行了基准测试，结果显示，最先进的语言模型在此问题上的推理能力存在显著差距，这表明处理冲突信息的推理能力并非语言模型与生俱来的。虽然通过微调可以提升性能，但效果仍然不佳。

# 1 Introduction  1 引言

A fundamental goal of AI since its early days has been automatically applying logical or deductive reasoning to draw new conclusions from existing knowledge [28, 20]. Since a large amount of knowledge is available in the form of natural language, tremendous effort has been put into developing models that can understand and reason over natural language [22, 40, 52, 30, 12] (see [33] for a survey). Recent years have seen substantial improvements in this direction thanks to advancements in pretrained language models (LMs) [8, 9] that can handle unstructured data more flexibly, combined with advanced prompting techniques [50, 29], and modular reasoning approaches [22, 12].  
自人工智能诞生之初，一个根本目标就是自动应用逻辑或演绎推理，从现有知识中得出新结论[28, 20]。由于大量知识以自然语言形式存在，人们投入巨大努力开发能够理解和推理自然语言的模型[22, 40, 52, 30, 12]（综述见[33]）。近年来，得益于预训练语言模型（LMs）[8, 9]的进步——这些模型能更灵活地处理非结构化数据，结合先进的提示技术[50, 29]以及模块化推理方法[22, 12]，该领域取得了显著进展。

We also note that while a large number of logical reasoning benchmarks provide all the knowledge needed to answer questions [46, 39, 40, 18], such benchmarks do not reflect common real-world scenarios where implicit background knowledge plays an important role in reasoning. Moreover, models that translate the textual examples into logical form and then leverage off-the-shelf solvers may excel on these datasets, which does not reflect the true performance of such models in real-world applications. For these reasons, in BoardgameQA only part of the knowledge required to solve the problem is provided as input to the LM; the missing knowledge has to come from the LM itself.

A board game theme allows us to create synthetic scenarios with complex defeasible rules to reason about that seem natural when stated in text and hence allows background commonsense world knowledge to also be used. We benchmark various LMs on BoardgameQA and measure their defeasible reasoning capacity. Most notably, our results reveal that LMs perform poorly when reasoning with conflicting sources, especially in the few-shot setting (compared to the finetuning setting) suggesting that preference understanding and defeasible reasoning capacities do not surface out-of-the-box in pretrained LMs. Secondly, we find that smaller LMs perform poorly when not all of the required information is provided as input. These results highlight a critical gap in the reasoning capacity of current LMs, considering that reasoning over contradicting and incomplete sets of information is a common scenario in many applications, and is key for developing robust AI systems.  
我们在 BoardgameQA 基准测试上评估了各种语言模型，并测量了它们的可废止推理能力。最值得注意的是，我们的结果显示，语言模型在处理冲突信息源时表现不佳，尤其是在少样本设置下（与微调设置相比），这表明偏好理解和可废止推理能力在预训练语言模型中并非开箱即用。其次，我们发现当并非所有必需信息都作为输入提供时，较小的语言模型表现较差。这些结果突显了当前语言模型在推理能力上的关键差距，考虑到在许多应用中，基于矛盾和不完整信息集进行推理是常见场景，并且对于开发稳健的人工智能系统至关重要。

Reasoning with conflicts: From the early days of AI, reasoning with conflicting information has been an important topic and many approaches have been developed to handle such conflicts [32, 31, 35]. The problem we study in this paper is an instance of defeasible reasoning [31, 19, 27] which has applications in various domains (especially in legal reasoning) [41, 16, 7] and has been argued to be one of the most important future directions in a recent survey of LM reasoning literature [54]. In defeasible reasoning, there are preferences over the rules and in the case of conflict between two rules, the conclusion from the higher preference rule is accepted. Previous work on defeasible reasoning with natural language has studied the problem of adjusting the probability of a conclusion based on new (single-hop) evidence [37, 26]. Our work extends this line of work by developing a dataset for multi-hop defeasible reasoning with preferences over sources.

# 4 The BoardgameQA Dataset  
4 BoardgameQA 数据集

We now describe how we create a dataset for measuring the ability of LMs in reasoning with conflicting inputs in a defeasible setup. Our dataset creation follows a backward story generation strategy similar to [53, 22]. Each example in the dataset contains a (defeasible) theory T(d) and a question q . The goal is to predict whether T(d)⊨q , or T(d)⊨ !q , or neither. Therefore, the label space for each question is {proved, disproved, unknown}. We next describe how we generate examples with the label proved; examples with the label disproved or unknown are created by modifying the examples with label proved.  
我们现在描述如何创建一个数据集，用于衡量语言模型在可废止设置中处理冲突输入时的推理能力。我们的数据集构建遵循类似于[53, 22]的后向故事生成策略。数据集中的每个示例包含一个（可废止的）理论 T(d) 和一个问题 q 。目标是预测 T(d)⊨q 、 T(d)⊨ !q ，或者两者都不是。因此，每个问题的标签空间为{已证明、已证伪、未知}。接下来，我们将描述如何生成标签为“已证明”的示例；标签为“已证伪”或“未知”的示例是通过修改标签为“已证明”的示例来创建的。

The facts of each theory describe the current state of a board game, the rules of each theory represent the rules of the board game, and the questions are about the game. In the design of BoardgameQA, we include several variables that can be used to sample examples with varying levels of difficulty with respect to several finer-grained properties (e.g., depth, number and types of conflicts).  
每个理论的事实描述了棋盘游戏的当前状态，每个理论的规则代表了棋盘游戏的规则，而问题则与游戏相关。在 BoardgameQA 的设计中，我们包含了多个变量，可用于根据多个细粒度属性（例如深度、冲突的数量和类型）来抽样不同难度级别的示例。

停止准则：每次我们对算法 16 中的函数进行递归调用时，示例的证明中都会增加一个额外的跳转。我们将停止准则设定为证明中的跳转次数。为此，我们在算法 16 中引入了一个参数 d ，它对应于证明中目标的最大跳转次数；每次进行递归调用时， d 会减少一。当算法以 d=0 被调用时，我们不会为输入问题 q 生成规则和子问题，而是简单地将 q 添加到我们的事实集合中。

Incomplete information: We generate examples with incomplete information where part of the knowledge should come from the LM (corresponds to rule type 5). For a question q in the theory generation phase, we sample sub-questions Q and rule r such that Q^​ can be derived based on Q and q can be derived from Q^​ and r . We then hide Q^​ from the model so the model has to derive it itself. We use a separate body of world knowledge, commonsense knowledge, mathematical, and orthography reasoning for generating Q and Q^​ (see Table 2 for a high-level description and Appendix C.2 for more details). For example, for the goal “the dog unites with the cat” we generate the sub-question “The dog is in Montreal.” and the rule “If the dog is in Canada, then the dog unites with the cat.”. Then, an extra reasoning step is needed from the model to recognize that Montreal is in Canada.  
信息不完整：我们生成信息不完整的示例，其中部分知识应来自语言模型（对应规则类型 5）。在理论生成阶段，对于问题 q ，我们采样子问题 Q 和规则 r ，使得 Q^​ 可以基于 Q 推导得出，而 q 可以从 Q^​ 和 r 推导得出。然后，我们向模型隐藏 Q^​ ，因此模型必须自行推导它。我们使用独立的世界知识、常识知识、数学和正字法推理来生成 Q 和 Q^​ （高级描述请参见表 2，更多细节请参见附录 C.2）。例如，对于目标“狗与猫联合”，我们生成子问题“狗在蒙特利尔。”和规则“如果狗在加拿大，那么狗与猫联合。”。然后，模型需要一个额外的推理步骤来识别蒙特利尔位于加拿大。

We generate sub-questions and rules that require extra knowledge and reasoning with probability pMissInfo; otherwise, we create sub-questions and rules that require no extra knowledge and reasoning. To make the problem more challenging, we only include some categories of extra knowledge and reasoning in the training set; this ensures that the models cannot simply learn the extra knowledge from the training set and use it in the test set.  
我们以概率 pMissInfo 生成需要额外知识和推理的子问题与规则；否则，我们创建无需额外知识和推理的子问题与规则。为了使问题更具挑战性，我们在训练集中仅包含部分类别的额外知识和推理；这确保了模型无法简单地从训练集中学习额外知识并在测试集中使用。

# 6 Conclusion  6 结论

In this work, we introduced BoardgameQA, a dataset for measuring the natural language reasoning ability of language models (LMs) in the presence of conflicting input sources. Our dataset furthermore includes scenarios in which the knowledge required for reasoning is only partially provided as input and additional information needs to come from the model itself. We tested several types of LMs on different variations of the dataset and observed that LMs perform poorly when reasoning with conflicting inputs. In the case of smaller models, the performance was also poor when additional knowledge from the LM is needed. Since reasoning over contradicting and incomplete sets of information is a common scenario in real-world applications, our results highlight an important gap in the reasoning capacity of current LMs. We hope our dataset can guide future work developing methodology to improve the reasoning ability of LMs under this setup, or finding alternative formulations of conflict resolution that better facilitate LM reasoning.  
在这项工作中，我们介绍了 BoardgameQA 数据集，用于衡量语言模型在存在冲突输入源时的自然语言推理能力。我们的数据集进一步包含了推理所需知识仅部分作为输入提供、额外信息需从模型自身获取的场景。我们在数据集的不同变体上测试了多种类型的语言模型，并观察到语言模型在处理冲突输入时表现不佳。对于较小规模的模型，当需要从语言模型中获取额外知识时，其表现同样较差。由于在现实应用中处理矛盾和不完整信息集是常见场景，我们的结果突显了当前语言模型推理能力的重要缺陷。我们希望该数据集能指导未来研究工作，开发改进语言模型在此设定下推理能力的方法，或寻找能更好促进语言模型推理的冲突解决替代方案。

Binary Classification: It was observed in [22] that reasoning with unknown labels is particularly challenging for few-shot LMs, because providing a natural chain-of-thought for unknown is difficult. To measure if the poor performance is merely due to the existence of examples with unknown label or due to conflict resolution being difficult for these models, we also created a binary version of the dataset for depths 1, 2, and 3 where only examples with proved and disproved labels are included.3 The results are reported in Figure 10. We overall see similar patterns as the binary case, except for some improvements for the T5 model on depth 2.  
二元分类：文献[22]指出，对于少样本语言模型而言，处理未知标签的推理尤为困难，因为为未知情况提供自然的思维链颇具挑战。为了探究性能不佳是否仅源于存在未知标签的示例，还是因为这些模型难以解决冲突，我们还创建了深度为 1、2 和 3 的数据集的二元版本，其中仅包含已证明和已证伪标签的示例。结果如图 10 所示。总体而言，我们观察到的模式与二元情况相似，除了 T5 模型在深度 2 上有所改进。

Examples of Model Failures: To showcase some of the reasons why models struggle with the BoardgameQA dataset, in Figures 12–21, we provide examples where the model generated wrong proofs. Some of the dominant error cases (showcased in the examples) include: 1- hallucinating or misunderstanding conflicts and preferences, 2- not being able to correctly fill in the incomplete information, 3- misunderstanding logical rules, 4- failing to prove both elements in a conjunction, 5- getting distracted by distracting facts and rules and going on a wrong proof path, and 6- being unfaithful to the provided facts and rules and changing them so a proof can be found in the case where no proof exists.  
模型失败案例：为展示模型在 BoardgameQA 数据集上表现不佳的部分原因，我们在图 12 至图 21 中提供了模型生成错误证明的示例。主要错误类型（通过示例展示）包括：1- 虚构或误解冲突与偏好，2- 无法正确补全不完整信息，3- 误解逻辑规则，4- 未能同时证明合取命题中的两个要素，5- 被干扰性事实和规则误导而走上错误证明路径，以及 6- 不忠实于给定事实和规则，在不存在证明的情况下篡改内容以强行构建证明。

Confusion Matrices: The confusion matrices for the model predictions with respect to the golden labels on the BoardgameQA dataset is provided in Figure 11 for various depths. One interesting observation is that for models tuned with proofs, while the model perform well at predicting unknown labels in lower depths, in higher depths they tend to generate proofs (with proved or disproved labels) even when the label is unknown (e.g., for examples with label unknown in depth 1 the prompt-tuned model predicts unknown for 268 instances, while in depth 2 it predicts unknown only for 2 instances). This may be because when the search space for a proof increases, LMs cannot verify all possible solutions and decide that the label is unknown. Instead, they start a path in the hopes that it ends in a proof.  
混淆矩阵：图 11 展示了 BoardgameQA 数据集上模型预测相对于黄金标签在不同深度下的混淆矩阵。一个有趣的发现是，对于使用证明进行调优的模型，虽然在较低深度下模型能很好地预测未知标签，但在较高深度下，即使标签未知，模型也倾向于生成证明（带有已证明或已证伪标签）（例如，在深度 1 中标签未知的示例中，提示调优模型对 268 个实例预测为未知，而在深度 2 中，仅对 2 个实例预测为未知）。这可能是因为当证明的搜索空间增加时，语言模型无法验证所有可能的解决方案，从而决定标签未知。相反，它们开始一条路径，希望最终能找到一个证明。

# C.1 Consistency of the Dataset  
C.1 数据集的一致性

A defeasible theory is called consistent if whenever a conflict arises, the preferences can be used to resolve the conflict. In BoardgameQA, we aim to produce consistent theories. To avoid inconsistencies and loops, each time we call the function in Algorithm 16, we only allow it to sample from the entities that have not been used in other rules and (sub-)questions. As an example, if we have a rule such as a∧b⇒c , then when we call the algorithm for a and b recursively, we use separate entities in the facts and rules produced for the sub-branch of a and for the sub-branch of b . This way, we ensure that when we derive new facts in the sub-branch of a , it does not defeat some of the derivations in the sub-branch of b (and the derivations in the later stages of the proof do not defeat the earlier derivations). That is because the the set of facts used in the rule bodies are for separate entities, and are therefore disjoint. We also apply defeasible reasoning on the final logical theory to ensure that the question can be derived from the theory.  
一个可废止理论被称为一致的，如果每当出现冲突时，偏好可以用来解决冲突。在 BoardgameQA 中，我们的目标是产生一致的理论。为了避免不一致和循环，每次我们调用算法 16 中的函数时，我们只允许它从尚未在其他规则和（子）问题中使用的实体中进行采样。例如，如果我们有一个规则如 a∧b⇒c ，那么当我们递归调用算法处理 a 和 b 时，我们为 a 的子分支和 b 的子分支生成的事实和规则中使用不同的实体。这样，我们确保当我们在 a 的子分支中推导新事实时，它不会击败 b 的子分支中的某些推导（并且证明后期阶段的推导不会击败早期的推导）。这是因为规则体中使用的实体集合是针对不同实体的，因此是不相交的。我们还在最终逻辑理论上应用可废止推理，以确保问题可以从该理论中推导出来。

# D Limitations and Negative Societal Impact  
D 局限性与负面社会影响

We identify the following limitations in our current work:  
我们在当前研究中发现以下局限性：

• Our dataset, in its current form, focuses primarily on deductive logical entailment, where the problem is a classification problem of whether a the answer to a question is proved, disproved, or unknown based on a theory, and the contradictions are also binary (i.e. one rule suggesting something is True and the other suggesting it is False). Future work can extend BoardgameQA and the analysis provided in this work to non-classification cases where 1- one needs to apply defeasible logical reasoning to answer questions such as “Who will be attacked by the dog?”, and 2- one needs to resolve non-binary conflicts where, e.g., one rule suggests “the dog is currently in Canada” and the other suggests “the dog is currently in Australia”.  
• 我们当前形式的数据集主要聚焦于演绎逻辑蕴含，其核心问题是一个分类任务：基于给定理论判断某个问题的答案是“被证明”、“被证伪”还是“未知”，且其中的矛盾也是二元的（例如，一条规则暗示某命题为真，另一条则暗示其为假）。未来的工作可以将 BoardgameQA 及本文提供的分析扩展到非分类场景，例如：1- 需要运用可废止逻辑推理来回答诸如“狗会攻击谁？”这类问题；2- 需要解决非二元冲突，例如一条规则指出“狗目前在加拿大”，而另一条规则指出“狗目前在澳大利亚”。

• The current work assumes the initial state (facts) and the rules of the game are small enough to be included in the prompt. Future work can extend BoardgameQA and our analyses to the cases where not all the facts and rules can be included in the prompt due to the limitation in the prompt length, and retrieval is required to retrieve relevant facts and rules.  
• 当前研究假设游戏的初始状态（事实）和规则规模足够小，可以完整包含在提示词中。未来的工作可以将 BoardgameQA 及我们的分析扩展到因提示词长度限制而无法包含所有事实和规则的情况，此时需要通过检索机制来获取相关的事实和规则。

• The current work is limited to deductive reasoning with the modus ponens rule; future work can expand BoardgameQA and the analysis provided in this paper to other types of rules such as proof by contradiction, disjunction elimination, etc (see [40]).  
• 当前研究仅限于使用肯定前件规则的演绎推理；未来工作可将 BoardgameQA 及本文提供的分析扩展至其他类型的推理规则，如反证法、析取消去等（参见[40]）。
